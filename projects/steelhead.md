# Overview

Steelhead AUV for UBC subbots. Worked on creating the IMU driver, autonomous navigation, and consolidating OpenCV, navigation, and PID controller nodes into a whole system.

We made it to the semi-finals in RoboSub 2025, where over 50 teams from around the world come together in California to compete with the underwater AUVs each university team has created, in various tasks.

2025 was also the best result in our team's history, and we definitely have a lot less funding than the other teams!
